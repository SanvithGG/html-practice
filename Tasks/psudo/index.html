WELCOME AND CHOOSE COFFEE MUG

A coffee mug is a cylindrical container used for drinking hot beverages such as coffee, tea, or hot chocolate. It usually has a sturdy handle on one side, allowing the user to hold it comfortably without touching the hot surface. Coffee mugs are commonly made from ceramic, glass, stainless steel, or plastic and come in various sizes, colors, and designs. Some mugs have printed quotes, artistic patterns, or company logos, making them both functional and decorative. They are widely used at home, in offices, and in cafés.
